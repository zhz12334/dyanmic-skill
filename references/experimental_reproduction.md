# Experimental Structural-Dynamics Reproduction

## Evidence to reconstruct
- specimen dimensions, material batch and tolerances
- fixture/support/preload/bolt torque/contact state
- sensor model, mass, sensitivity, orientation and coordinates
- actuator/impact hammer/shaker and force transducer
- DAQ hardware, sample rate, filters, trigger, record length
- windows, overlap, averaging, FRF estimator and coherence
- calibration records
- environment/temperature/operating state
- repeated trials and uncertainty
- channel map and sign/coordinate conventions

## Experimental modes
- exact experiment rerun
- disclosed-data analysis reproduction
- simulation-to-experiment reconstruction

Do not claim an experimental reproduction when only a simulation was calibrated to the published experiment.
